# Captions: assertiveness sweep (E4, tag `sweep`)

## fig_assertiveness

**Fig. (fig_assertiveness).** Micro-macro link: how the SUMO lane-change assertiveness $A$ (lcAssertive) enters the macroscopic model. All panels: $u_\xi=15$ m/s, slow window $[260,740]$ s, 5 repetitions per point, $q_{in}=2500$ veh/h (filled circles) and $2000$ veh/h (open squares), $A$ on a logarithmic axis. (a) Measured overtaking flow $\omega_\xi$ past the controlled vehicle (mean over repetitions, shading = min/max), the analog of ECC22 Fig. 1; dashed lines are the free-flow inverse reference $\omega_0(q_{in})=q_{in}-\rho_{in}u_\xi$ with $\rho_{in}=q_{in}/v_f$ ($\omega_0=926$ and $1158$ veh/h). $\omega_\xi$ rises steeply from $A=1$ to $A=2$, peaks at $A=2.25$ (886 veh/h, $q_{in}=2000$) and $A=2.5$ (888 veh/h, $q_{in}=2500$), then decays slowly to 865 and 845 veh/h at $A=10$. For $A\ge2$ the two inflows give the same overtaking flow to within 2%: the bottleneck throughput is capacity-limited, not demand-limited, at $q_{in}=2500$ veh/h, whereas at $q_{in}=2000$ veh/h the peak reaches 96% of $\omega_0$. (b) Poisson-exposure maximum-likelihood estimates of the capture coefficient $\hat\kappa_c$ ($\ell f$ form, red) and the release coefficient $\hat\kappa_r$ (green), pooled over the 5 repetitions with the model speed difference $\Delta v(\rho)$; error bars are 95% confidence intervals, downward triangles are rule-of-three upper bounds where no release was observed. At $q_{in}=2500$ veh/h $\hat\kappa_r$ rises from an upper bound $1.7\times10^{-5}$ 1/veh (0 releases in 5 runs) at $A=1$ to $1.6\times10^{-2}$ 1/veh at $A=10$ (more than 911-fold), with the steepest rise between $A=1.5$ and $A=2.25$ ($3.1\times10^{-5}$ to $4.8\times10^{-3}$ 1/veh); $\hat\kappa_c$ stays within $[2.6\times10^{-2},\,5.2\times10^{-2}]$ 1/veh over the whole sweep. (c) Synchronized occupancy $\chi_s$, the fraction of vehicle-hours in the queue zone spent in the caught (synchronized) state; it falls from 0.61 ($A=1$) to 0.02 ($A=10$) at $q_{in}=2500$ veh/h and from 0.53 to 0.00 at $2000$ veh/h, mirroring the $\hat\kappa_r$ transition of panel (b).
